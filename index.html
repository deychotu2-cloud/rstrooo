Our Story

A Little Place With A Big Bengali Heart

Cafe Adda was created with a simple idea — to bring the authentic taste of Bengal to every table.

From traditional Bengali recipes to festive favourites, every dish is prepared with carefully selected ingredients and plenty of love.

Come sit with us, enjoy good food and have a little adda.

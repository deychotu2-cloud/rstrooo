About Us

The Taste of Bengal

Cafe Adda is a Bengali restaurant inspired by the traditional flavours of West Bengal.

From delicious chicken dishes to traditional Bengali sweets, every dish is prepared with care and authentic flavours.

Our goal is simple — good food, warm hospitality and a place where everyone can enjoy a true Bengali food experience.
